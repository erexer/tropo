Tropo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10/13/2026. Developer: erexer. Contact: tropodev@gmail.com.

Tropo provides weather forecasts, air quality, saved locations, and a home-screen widget. The app is free and non-commercial, with no advertising, subscriptions, in-app purchases, user accounts, or app analytics SDK.

Location And Weather Requests

You can search for a city without granting device-location permission. When you tap Use current location, Tropo requests foreground precise or approximate location permission and obtains a recent one-shot position. It does not request background-location permission or continuously track your position. Android's location and geocoding services may process that position under the policies of your device/platform providers.

Tropo sends the latitude and longitude of the selected or saved location directly to Open-Meteo to request weather and air-quality data. These may be precise device coordinates when you choose current location. City search terms are sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. Requests use HTTPS. The provider also receives your IP address and ordinary request metadata.

Open-Meteo states that technical logs may contain IP addresses and geographic coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. This is provider-controlled retention, not a guarantee that requests are ephemeral. See Open-Meteo's terms and privacy notice.

Data On Your Device

Tropo stores selected/saved locations, names and coordinates, current/cached weather, forecasts, air quality, and display preferences locally. The widget and scheduled refreshes may request weather for the stored selected location; they do not obtain a new device position in the background.

Removing a favorite unpins it but does not necessarily erase previously cached location/weather records. Local records remain until Android app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Backup And Device Transfer

App backup is enabled. Depending on your Android/device settings, preferences, saved locations including coordinates, and cached weather may be included in system cloud backups or device transfers. On Android 12 and later, cloud backup is restricted to transports reporting encryption capabilities. Backup availability, security, retention, and restoration are controlled by Android and your backup provider. Backups may survive uninstall and may restore app data later.

You can manage or disable backup through your device/account settings. Clearing app storage does not necessarily erase provider-held backups or Open-Meteo logs.

Sharing And Support

Tropo has no developer-operated weather server and does not sell your data or use it for advertising. Information is sent to the weather/geocoding providers described above to deliver requested features. If you email support, your email address and message are used to respond, handled by the email service, and retained as needed for support or applicable obligations. Do not send passwords, signing keys, or sensitive information in support messages.

Report an issue in Settings creates an editable email draft with reproduction prompts, app version and build code, and Android version/API level. You can review, edit, or remove this information before sending through your email app. Reports are never sent automatically. Coordinates, favorites, device identifiers, and logs are not attached automatically. You may also choose to copy the report or support address to your device clipboard.

Your Choices

  • Use city search instead of device location.
  • Revoke location permission in Android app settings.
  • Remove favorites, clear app storage, or uninstall the app to control local records.
  • Manage backups through your device/account provider.
  • Contact support about developer-held support messages. Provider logs/backups remain subject to those providers' policies.

Policy Changes

This page will be updated when app behavior or relevant practices change. The last-updated date identifies the latest policy version.
